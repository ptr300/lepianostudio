About me

Peter Le is a pianist and teacher in the city of Austin. He graduated from the University of Texas at Austin with a Bachelor of Music degree in Music Education, studying with Laurie Scott, Bob Duke, and Margarita Denenburg. At the University of Texas, he studied piano under Andrew Brownell, Patti Wolf, and James Maverick. While in his hometown of Waco, his private teachers included Jeanenne Bezerra and Jane Abbott-Kirk from Baylor/University of North Texas. He began his journey on the piano at age six and continued studying it ever since then, all the way into college.

While at the University of Texas, Peter has gained much teaching experience, from teaching privately at local Austin schools to teaching general music at elementary schools to teaching right at UT Austin in their highly esteemed piano program, Piano Project. After graduating, he joined Harmony Music Lessons to continue refining his craft. There, he taught his students a variety of music to perform for semesterly studio recitals, or simply for personal enjoyment. Today, he takes the skills he learned to teach his own studio, always striving to achieve more.

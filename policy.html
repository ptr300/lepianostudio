Studio policy

About the Teacher

During lessons, I will prioritize maintaining a safe and positive learning environment that promotes the development of a joyful and healthy relationship with music and piano in every student. This is a space where everyone should feel comfortable and respected.

Tuition and Fees

Tuition is divided into equal monthly payments, based on the academic school year; it is due at the beginning of each month. Payment is non-refundable and is accepted through cash, check, or an online method of transfer, such as Zelle or Venmo. All students will get the same number of lessons per month, over the school year. Generally, it is expected to have 4 lessons each month. Tuition for each month will remain the same regardless of how many lessons were had in a month. Tuition is prorated for the month if a student starts in the middle of a month. There is an additional late fee of $5 if payment is not received by the first week of each month.

  • 30-minute lessons: $50/lesson = $200/month
  • 45-minute lessons: $65/lesson = $260/month
  • 60-minute lessons: $80/lesson = $320/month

Please note that this does not include fees for books and materials used for lessons, as well as paid events that a student partakes in, including any Studio Recital. When necessary, I will obtain the required materials to be used in lessons and will add the purchase cost to the next month’s tuition. In addition, students can request music of any kind to play (such as pop/film/video game songs), and I will arrange it in a way that specifically tailors to their reading level, strengths, and weaknesses! Arrangements found online or even by credible publishers are often arranged in a way that doesn’t fit the average student well. Furthermore, students will usually want one-off songs that don’t warrant purchasing an entire book filled with other music they wouldn’t even touch; buying songs individually is also an option, but not a cost-efficient one. For this reason, all arrangements I make for the students are both generally the best fit for them and free!

Lessons and Studio Recital

Students are offered individual lessons ranging from 30, 45, or 60-minute lessons. Lesson times are recommended for each age group as follows:

  • Beginners age 6-9: 30-45 minutes
  • Intermediate age 10 and up: 45-60 minutes
  • Teens and adults: 60 minutes

The lesson contents will contribute to developing healthy technique and a sense of musicianship for pianists of all skill levels, with a focus on Classical training. Other genres of music can be worked with on an individual basis, depending on students’ requests or musical needs. Along with this, students will also be taught music theory to help them understand what they play and how it can be applied to anything they play. Students are also expected to perform in Studio recitals. For the 2026/2027 school year, there will be a Spring Recital held in May. These recitals help build a sense of community in students and open up opportunities for all musicians, regardless of age or ability, to express and share their music with everyone! Studio recitals are semi-formal and low-stakes to help gain performance experience in a supportive environment.

Student Expectations and Homework

The student should arrive on time with all the necessary materials, books, and music. Any assigned homework is to be completed or worked on prior to each lesson. Daily practice is imperative to making progress and developing consistency. Just 10 or 15 minutes, even on their busiest days, can go an extremely long way! Establishing a routine is very important for maintaining their skills, and is the responsibility of both the student AND parent to do so. Acoustic instruments are not required, but highly recommended at home for the proper development of technique. The student is to be respectful and cooperative. If any difficulties arise, they will receive an initial warning; afterward, the teacher will dismiss the student as necessary. Personal hygiene must be maintained, including washing hands before lessons and keeping their nails trimmed short.

Attendance and Missed Lessons

As lessons are paid by the month, any missed lesson is already paid regardless of whether or not they were made up. Make-ups can be scheduled, although there is no guarantee that there will be off-schedule lessons if the student/parent canceled. Many unexpected things can happen over the semester, however! One lesson per semester can be offered as a guaranteed make-up. Conversely, if the teacher cancels for any reason, then a make-up will be scheduled at a mutually convenient time. If the student is feeling unwell/sick, they should not have a lesson until they are fully recovered. The teacher may choose to send a student home if they arrive ill. Lessons are considered “no-shows” if I arrive at the student’s house and no one answers for at least 10-15 minutes, and if no advance notice was given. These missed lessons cannot be refunded nor rescheduled for make-up. Lessons are not taught on the following holiday weeks: Thanksgiving, Winter Break, and Spring Break.

Summer Lessons

Everyone needs to have fun and take a break! It is important, however, that students do not go an entire 3 months without playing the piano. Over the entire summer break, we will coordinate to have a minimum of 6 lessons; it does not have to be on a regular weekly schedule, and they can also choose to take more than one lesson in a given week - flexibility is key during this time. You may also request to have more than 6 lessons. We can also communicate to fit around any hectic vacation/travel schedule. Payment for summer lessons will be equivalent to 1.5 months’ worth of regular tuition, unless you requested to have extra lessons.

Termination of Lessons

The teacher has the right to dismiss any difficult and uncooperative student and terminate their lessons. They may also be terminated if tuition consistently goes unpaid or excessive missed lessons occur. If the student and/or parent requests to end lessons for whatever reason, they must give 30 days of notice. Lessons during that time are still expected to be given and paid for. For example, if your student had a lesson on September 15th and you submitted a request to stop taking lessons also on that day, the student will have lessons until October 15th.

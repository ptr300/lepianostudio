Studio policy

About the Teacher

During lessons, I will prioritize maintaining a safe and positive learning environment that promotes the development of a joyful and healthy relationship with music and piano in every student. This is a space where everyone should feel comfortable and respected.

Student Expectations and Homework

The student should arrive on time with all the necessary materials, books, and music. Any assigned homework is to be completed or worked on prior to each lesson. Daily practice is imperative to making progress and developing consistency. Acoustic instruments are highly recommended home for the proper development of technique. The student is to be respectful and cooperative. If any difficulties arise, they will receive an initial warning; afterward, the teacher will dismiss the student as necessary. Personal hygiene must be maintained, including washing hands before lessons and keeping their nails trimmed short.

Lessons and Studio Recital

Students are offered individual lessons ranging from 30, 45, or 60-minute lessons. Lesson times are recommended for each age group as follows:

  • Beginners age 6-9: 30-45 minutes
  • Intermediate age 10 and up: 45-60 minutes
  • Teens and adults: 60 minutes

The lesson contents will contribute to developing healthy technique and a sense of musicianship for pianists of all skill levels, with a focus on Classical training. Other genres of music can be worked with on an individual basis, depending on students’ requests or musical needs. Along with this, students will also be taught music theory to help them understand what they play and how it shows up in our repertoire. Students are expected to perform in two Studio recitals - a Winter Recital held in December and a Spring recital held in May. These recitals help build a sense of community in students and open up opportunities for all musicians, regardless of age or ability, to express and share their music with everyone! Studio recitals are semi-formal and low-stakes to help gain performance experience in a supportive environment.

Tuition and Fees

Tuition is divided equally across 9 months, based on the academic school year; it is due at the beginning of each month. Payment is non-refundable and is accepted through cash, check, or an online method of transfer, such as Zelle or Venmo. The rate of tuition also varies depending on whether 30, 45, or 60-minute lessons are selected as the regular lesson timeslot per week. There is an additional late fee of $5 per week, up to $20 if payment is not received by the first lesson of each month.

Please note that this does not include fees for books and materials used for lessons, as well as paid events that a student partakes in. The teacher will purchase the required materials to be used in lessons and will bill the student separately.

Attendance and Missed Lessons

As lessons are paid by the month, any missed lesson is already paid regardless of whether or not they were made up. Make-ups can be scheduled, although there is no guarantee that there will be off-schedule lessons if the student/parent canceled. If the teacher cancels for any reason, then a make-up will be scheduled at a mutually convenient time. If the student is feeling unwell/sick, they should not have a lesson until they are fully recovered. The teacher may choose to send a student home if they arrive ill. Lessons are not taught on the following holiday weeks: Thanksgiving, Winter Break, and Spring Break. Summer lessons can be arranged on an individual basis.

Summer Lessons

Everyone needs to have fun and take a break! It is important, however, that students do not go an entire 3 months without playing the piano. Over the span of June and July, the students should take 4 lessons; it does not have to be at a regular weekly schedule and they can also choose to take more than one lesson in a given week. We will coordinate to fit around any hectic vacation/travel schedule. Payment for summer lessons will be equivalent to one month’s worth of tuition of a regular school year.

Termination of Lessons

The teacher has the right to dismiss any difficult and uncooperative student and terminate their lessons. They may also be terminated if tuition consistently goes unpaid or excessive missed lessons occur. If the student requests to end lessons for whatever reason, they must give a 1 month’s notice. Tuition is not refunded for lost lessons of a month in this regard.
